    Bounding the Moon and Mars

    PSI holds microgravity flight data and normal-gravity ground data, but no partial-gravity combustion data yet.

    Microgravity and 1-g results bracket lunar (0.17 g) and Martian (0.38 g) behavior. E-FIELD's ion wind adds a body force that mimics partial gravity; LUCI and FM2 are expected to add true lunar-g data.
